## Hello, I’m Eitan.

I’m interested in what happens beneath the surface of software: how programs behave, how protections are implemented, and how complex code can become easier to understand.

This website brings together my technical writing, published vulnerability disclosures, and selected public documents.

## What you’ll find here

**Research** follows an idea through its technical details, including code, diagrams, and the limitations of an approach. **Disclosures** document published security findings and remediation. **Documents** collect selected designation letters and redacted assessment summaries as they become available.

Older research is labeled with its original publication date and context. Examples and tool behavior in historical articles reflect the versions used at the time.
